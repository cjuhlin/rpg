# Session Summary: Ash, Adolescents, and Authorities

## ⚓ The Voyage Begins

The crew joined forces with [[Dodson]], who has lent his ship to the crew. The primary objective is to find Dodson's former [[Captain Gravestock]], who holds the other half of the treasure map.

## ⚖️ Trade & Rumors

- **The French Deal:** Met a French merchant ship. Successfully sold **25lbs of Ash** for **333s**.
- **The Rumor:** Heard that Dodson's Captain is being detained by [[The Redcoats]] in the city for smuggling Ash.

## 🧔 The "Pirate" Encounter

Encountered a ship flying a Jolly Roger. The crew appeared to be 10 friendly pirates led by a bearded captain.

- **The Twist:** The "pirates" are actually **[[The Ash Teens]]** wearing fake beards.
- **The Situation:** They are trying to save their mother from the Black Plague. They are being squeezed for money by **[[Dr. House]]**.
- **The Result:** The crew convinced them to join and sold them **25lbs of Ash** (despite their mother's warnings).

## 💂 Infiltrating the City

- **False Flag:** The crew flew a **British Flag** to avoid being shot on sight by the harbor guards.
- **The Docks:** Paid the fee to **[[Charles Entwistle]]**.
- **The Bribe:** One crew member bribed the Harbormaster to secure a custom weapon.

## 🕵️ The Investigation

[[The Redcoats]] confronted the crew at the docks, investigating Ash-related deaths at a local tavern.

- **The Distraction:** A crew member fumbled and fell to distract the guards.
- **The Forgery:** Another member sprinted to [[Charles Entwistle]] and bribed him to falsify the ship's logs just in time.
- **The Accusation:** The crew accused [[Dr. House]] of scamming people, but the guards demanded proof.

**Current Status:** Safe (for now), but under investigation.
