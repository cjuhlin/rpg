# Silent Angel

## Description

A shipwreck located deep within the jungle ruins of [[Eel Island]]. The ship was discovered hanging upside down, precariously suspended by thick jungle vines.

## Points of Interest

- **The Captain's Quarters:** The door was jammed but eventually forced open. Inside was the skeletal body of the former captain, with a wooden spoon jammed in its eye socket. Upon removing the spoon, the skeleton animated.
- **The Loot:** The crew recovered the first half of a **[[Treasure map]]**, silver, weapons, and ammo from the wreck.
