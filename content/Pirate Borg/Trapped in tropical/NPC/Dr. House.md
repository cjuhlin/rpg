# Dr. House

> "Without evidence, the Doctor is just doing his job." — A weary Redcoat.

- **Appearance:** Looks like a classic plague doctor (bird mask, heavy robes). He carries a silver-topped cane.
- **Behavior:** Known to be "treating" the Black Plague, but the crew suspects he is scamming desperate families.
- **Connections:** Currently "treating" the mother of **[[The Ash Teens]]**.
- **Player Knowledge:** The crew has openly accused him of fraud to the authorities.
