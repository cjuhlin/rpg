# The Ash Teens

- **Composition:** A group of 10 teenagers.
- **Appearance:** They wear fake horsehair beards to look like hardened pirates.
- **Motivation:** Their mother is sick with the Black Plague. They are trying to raise money for her "cure."
- **Relationship:** They have joined the player's crew and recently purchased **25lbs of Ash** from the players.
- **Moral Compass:** Their mother told them not to deal with drugs, a rule the players helped them break.
