# Mr. Oxley

- **Role:** Former Bosun / Traitor
- **Status:** Alive
- **Associations:** The Crew (Former Bosun)

## Description & Motivations

- **The Betrayal:** He was the bosun of the crew but betrayed them upon arriving at [[Eel Island]].
- **The Theft:** While the crew was distracted in the jungle and ambushed, he stole their ship, the _[[Wharf rat]]_, and sailed away toward the horizon, leaving them stranded.
